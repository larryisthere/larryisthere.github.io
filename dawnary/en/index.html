Dawnary

A quiet, private place for everyday writing.

Dawnary is a journal designed for writing over the long term. Open it, write, and move on with your day. Your entry saves automatically.

Download on the App Store Support

Write it down, then keep living.

Dawnary keeps the focus on your words and the moment. An entry can hold photos, or simply a few sentences.

Your journal stays on your device and works fully offline. When you want to continue writing across devices, you can choose to sync through iCloud.

Features

  • Everyday writingStart writing right away, and your entry saves automatically as you go.
  • Words and photosAdd photos between your words to keep a fuller record of everyday life.
  • Tags and templatesOrganize entries with tags and start familiar routines with templates.
  • SearchSearch by text and tags to find the entries you want to read again.
  • Calendar and On This DayBrowse entries by date and revisit the same day in past years.
  • Statistics and badgesSee word counts, writing days, and streaks, with badges marking your progress.
  • Share cardsTurn a selected entry into a share card to take a chosen moment beyond your journal.
  • Local firstYour journal stays on your device, works offline, and can optionally sync through iCloud.
  • Live MirrorEnable Live Mirror to keep local Markdown copies of your entries for use with AI tools you trust.
  • Native on MacKeep writing and organizing on Mac with a native editor, sidebar, and keyboard shortcuts.
