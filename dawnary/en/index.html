Dawnary

A quiet, private place for everyday writing.

Dawnary is a journal designed for writing over the long term. Open it, write, and move on with your day—your entry saves automatically.

View on the App Store Support

Write it down, then keep living.

Dawnary keeps the focus on your words and the moment. An entry can hold photos, or simply a few sentences.

Your journal stays on your device and works fully offline. When you want to continue writing across devices, you can choose to sync through iCloud.

Features

  • Focused writingA native editor, automatic saving, and calm reading typography.
  • Words and photosAdd photos between paragraphs and keep a fuller record of the day.
  • Search and revisitFind past entries through full-text search, the calendar, and On This Day.
  • Local firstUse Dawnary without an account, fully offline, with optional iCloud sync.

Dawnary is available for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
