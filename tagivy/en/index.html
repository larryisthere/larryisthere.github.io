Tagivy

Turn videos scattered across folders into a library of your own.

Tagivy is a native video tag manager for macOS. It reads videos where they already live, then gives you hierarchical tags and combined filters to organize them.

Download for Mac View source

macOS 14+ · Apple silicon and Intel · Free and open source

Your videos stay where they are. You define the order.

Tagivy brings videos from multiple folders, external drives, and projects into one library. Tags can be hierarchical, or begin with the folder structure you already have.

The app accesses original videos as read-only files. It does not move, rename, modify, or delete them. The library index, tags, and thumbnails stay on your Mac, and Tagivy makes no network requests by default.

Features

  • Hierarchical tagsBuild multi-level tag trees around people, projects, sources, or status.
  • Combined filtersCombine tags, filenames, dates, and media properties, then save useful views.
  • One libraryBrowse videos across locations while preserving the folders you already use.
  • Offline organizationKeep browsing thumbnails, searching, and editing tags while an external drive is disconnected.
  • New video scansCheck folders you imported for new videos, preview them, and import only the ones you choose.
  • App lockOptionally lock the library with macOS authentication, including after a period of inactivity.

Tagivy requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Tagivy was previously called FileFacet. It uses a new app identity and does not import an existing FileFacet library automatically.
