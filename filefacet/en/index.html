FileFacet

Turn videos scattered across folders into a library of your own.

FileFacet is a native video tag manager for macOS. It reads videos where they already live, then gives you hierarchical tags and combined filters to organize them.

Download for Mac View source

Your videos stay where they are. You define the order.

FileFacet brings videos from multiple folders, external drives, and projects into one library. Tags can be hierarchical, or begin with the folder structure you already have.

The app accesses original videos as read-only files. It does not move, rename, modify, or delete them. The library index, tags, and thumbnails stay on your Mac, and FileFacet makes no network requests by default.

Features

  • Hierarchical tagsBuild multi-level tag trees around people, projects, sources, or status.
  • Combined filtersCombine tags, filenames, dates, and media properties, then save useful views.
  • One libraryBrowse videos across locations while preserving the folders you already use.
  • Offline organizationKeep browsing thumbnails, searching, and editing tags while an external drive is disconnected.

FileFacet requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
