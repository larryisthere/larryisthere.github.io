Members can write new diaries and edit them. Everything you've already written can always be read, searched, synced and exported, member or not.

## Ways to get membership

### Your first month

Membership starts the first time you use a version of Dawnary that has it. It lasts at least 30 days, then runs to the end of that month.

### Write on 7 days a month

Write on 7 days in a calendar month and the next month is yours. Length doesn't matter, and the days don't need to be in a row. The seven dots on Get Membership Free show this month's progress.

### Share Dawnary

Share how you journal with Dawnary on Xiaohongshu (RED) to get membership. The event runs in rounds; see the [sharing event](/dawnary-share-en.html) for this round's requirements and dates.

### One year

One purchase, valid for a year.

- It's not a subscription. It never renews, and you're never charged when it ends.
- Buy another while you're a member and the new year starts when the current membership ends, so nothing is lost.
- The price shown in the app is the one that applies.

### Lifetime

One purchase, valid forever. Nothing more to do.

## Your devices

Purchases and redeemed codes belong to your Apple Account, so membership works on every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to it. On a new device, tap Restore Purchases on Get Membership Free.

## When membership ends

- Everything you've written can still be read, searched, synced, exported and deleted.
- New diaries can't be started, and existing ones open read-only.
- Nothing you're in the middle of writing is lost when membership ends.
- Writing on 7 days, sharing or buying brings it back.

Before it ends, Me and Get Membership Free show the date. If notifications are on, one reminder also arrives 3 days before.

## Redeem a code

Open Dawnary, go to Me → Get Membership Free → Redeem Code and enter the code. Codes come from the App Store and expire, so please redeem soon after you receive one.
