Share how you journal with Dawnary on Xiaohongshu (RED) and get **one year of membership**.

## How to join

1. Post a note about Dawnary on Xiaohongshu: why you keep a diary, what you like about Dawnary. Words, screenshots or photos all work.
2. Mention the official Dawnary account in the note (Xiaohongshu ID: **TBD**).
3. After posting, send the note's link to the official account by direct message.
4. Within 3 business days we'll reply with a one-year membership code.

## How to redeem

Open Dawnary, go to Me → Perks → Redeem Code and enter the code. It's in the same place on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Codes come from the App Store. Once redeemed, membership belongs to your Apple Account and works on every device signed in to it.

## Notes

- One code per Apple Account.
- Likes, saves and views don't matter. Just share honestly.
- The one-year membership doesn't renew, and you're never charged when it ends.
- When membership ends, every diary you've written can still be read, searched, synced and exported.
- Codes expire; the App Store redeem page shows the date. Please redeem soon after you receive one.
- The event may change or end; this page is the reference.
